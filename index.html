The kitchen

Named for the person still at the stove.

Nina Chen opened the room after ten years in hotel kitchens, with one rule: the dumplings get pleated by hand, and the duck finishes in the oven until the skin crackles. The dining room is dark wood and white linen. The cooking is louder.

Suppliers are close — ducks from a Hudson Valley farm, tofu pressed the morning of service, scallions from the market two blocks over. Tea is poured before the menus land.
